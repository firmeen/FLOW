# Contractors & Freelancers

Controls independent contractor engagements from scope approval and agreement through SOW, access, deliverables, acceptance, invoice/payment, IP/source handover, and offboarding.

## Core chain
```text
Need/scope approved → contractor selected → contractor agreement + SOW → NDA/IP/security terms → limited access → milestone delivery → objective acceptance evidence → invoice → tax/WHT/payment → final source/docs/credential handover → access revocation → IP completeness check → archive
```

## Principles
- distinguish contractor structure from employment with professional advice where needed;
- every material engagement has a measurable SOW;
- payment should link to accepted deliverables/milestones;
- least-privilege access expires with business need;
- all company source/design/docs and IP rights must be handed over before closure;
- confidential contractor identity/payment/tax files stay outside Git.
