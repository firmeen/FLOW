# Contractor Invoices & Payments

Links contractor billing to executed agreement/SOW, accepted milestones, tax/WHT treatment, approval, bank evidence, and accounting.

## Payment packet
```text
Contractor agreement + SOW
+ accepted milestone/deliverable
+ contractor invoice
+ tax/WHT classification
+ payment approval
+ bank/payment evidence
+ certificate/filing where applicable
+ accounting reference
```

## Controls
Verify invoice identity/bank details, duplicate invoice, milestone amount, currency, taxes/WHT, approval threshold, remaining contract value, and outstanding handover conditions.

## Completion gate
Payment is not complete until provider/bank status, tax/WHT action, accounting posting, and reconciliation are resolved.
