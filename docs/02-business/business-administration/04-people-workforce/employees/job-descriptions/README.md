# Job Descriptions

Defines role purpose, responsibilities, decision authority, reporting line, required competencies, systems/access profile, and material data/security obligations without trying to replace the employment agreement.

## Required sections
Role title, business purpose, reporting line, key outcomes/responsibilities, decision rights, recurring tasks, collaboration interfaces, systems/data access expected, confidentiality/security responsibilities, required skills, performance-context notes, and review date.

## Change triggers
Promotion, team restructure, material responsibility change, new privileged access, regulatory/privacy responsibilities, or repeated mismatch between actual work and documented role.

## Workflow
```text
Role need → draft → manager/business review → HR/legal consistency check where required → approval → employee acknowledgement where appropriate → access/authority matrix alignment → periodic review
```

Job descriptions should not silently expand into legal obligations or compensation terms that belong in formal employment amendments.
