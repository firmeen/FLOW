# Offer & Employment

Controls the commercial offer and legally operative employment terms for a new employee.

## Pre-offer inputs
Approved role/headcount, employment type, compensation range, budget, reporting line, target start date, work location/mode, expected schedule where relevant, benefits/leave structure, equipment/access requirements, and hiring approval.

## Employment agreement areas
Employer entity, position/duties, place/mode of work, working schedule, salary/payment timing, probation if used, leave/benefits, confidentiality, IP, systems/equipment, policies, conflicts/outside work, and termination provisions consistent with applicable law.

## Workflow
```text
Candidate selected → compensation/role approval → offer prepared → review → offer accepted → employment agreement finalized → signatures → effective date → employee master/payroll/onboarding tasks created
```

## Completion gate
Do not provision material access solely from a verbal offer. The actual employment relationship and start date must be confirmed under the approved process.
