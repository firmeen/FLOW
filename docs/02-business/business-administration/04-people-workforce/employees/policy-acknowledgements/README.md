# Employee Policy Acknowledgements

Controls evidence that employees received, reviewed, and where required acknowledged applicable company policies and later material revisions.

## Typical policies
Information security, acceptable use, confidentiality, privacy/data handling, access control, remote work/device use, expense policy, incident reporting, code/open-source policy, leave/HR policies, and other role-specific controls.

## Evidence fields
Employee, policy ID/version, effective date, acknowledgement text, delivery method, acknowledged timestamp, exception/question, training reference if applicable, and superseded version.

## Workflow
```text
Policy approved → affected population identified → communication/training → acknowledgement collected → missing acknowledgements escalated → record retained → material revision triggers new rollout
```

Acknowledgement evidence does not replace the need to operate the policy controls.
