# Checklists

Contains objective, repeatable completion gates for recurring or high-risk business events.

## Checklist design standard
Every item should be verifiable, name the expected evidence or outcome, and avoid vague instructions such as “check legal stuff.” Where ownership matters, identify responsible role. Where timing matters, include due/lead time. Where an item is conditional, state the condition.

Good item:
```text
- Confirm current company certificate satisfies the counterparty's required freshness window and record secure evidence link.
```

Weak item:
```text
- Check company docs.
```

## Completion model
A checked box is not enough if evidence is missing. High-risk checklists should record preparer, reviewer, date, exceptions, deferred actions, and final approval.

## Maintenance
Review checklist content after incidents, audit findings, process changes, new providers, legal/tax changes, or repeated exceptions.
