# Year-End Checklist

Builds on monthly close to prepare annual financial, tax, corporate, audit, and evidence requirements.

## Checklist
- [ ] All monthly reconciliations current and old exceptions resolved/escalated.
- [ ] Revenue/AR completeness reviewed.
- [ ] Expenses/AP and supporting documents complete.
- [ ] Bank/provider confirmations/reconciliations final.
- [ ] Fixed-asset register/depreciation support complete.
- [ ] Payroll/statutory/tax balances reconciled.
- [ ] WHT certificates/credits complete or exception list approved.
- [ ] VAT and other tax control accounts reconcile when applicable.
- [ ] Accruals/prepayments/related-party/founder advances reviewed.
- [ ] Corporate income-tax working file reconciles to accounts.
- [ ] Financial statements/statutory approvals and filing requirements scheduled/completed as applicable.
- [ ] Corporate registers/current particulars reviewed.
- [ ] Annual access/vendor/policy/contract review obligations checked.
- [ ] Adviser/auditor questions tracked to closure.
- [ ] Final filed/approved statements and returns archived securely.
- [ ] Next-year recurring compliance calendar seeded.

Do not reconstruct the year from bank statements if monthly evidence exists; reconcile to controlled ledgers and source records.
