# Month-End Checklist

Coordinates finance/accounting/tax evidence into a repeatable monthly close.

## Checklist
- [ ] Period cut-off and missing-document deadline communicated.
- [ ] Customer invoices, credits, refunds, and AR updated.
- [ ] Vendor invoices, expenses, and AP updated.
- [ ] Every bank account reconciled.
- [ ] Every payment provider/settlement reconciled.
- [ ] AR ageing and collection exceptions reviewed.
- [ ] AP ageing/due payments reviewed.
- [ ] WHT issued/received exceptions reviewed.
- [ ] VAT input/output and tax-document exceptions reviewed when applicable.
- [ ] Payroll and statutory liabilities reconciled.
- [ ] Fixed assets and material prepaid/accrued items updated.
- [ ] Unusual balances/manual journals reviewed.
- [ ] Management P&L/balance-sheet/cash view produced.
- [ ] Adjustments approved and logged.
- [ ] Tax filing references/status linked.
- [ ] Reviewer sign-off and carry-forward exception list completed.

A close with unexplained balancing items is not a completed close.
