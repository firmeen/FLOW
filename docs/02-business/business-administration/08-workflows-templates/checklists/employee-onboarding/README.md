# Employee Onboarding Checklist

Verifies legal employment, payroll, security, equipment, policy, and ownership controls before and during start.

## Checklist
- [ ] Headcount/role/budget approved.
- [ ] Offer and employment terms accepted/executed.
- [ ] Job description/current reporting line recorded.
- [ ] Employee privacy information delivered.
- [ ] Confidentiality/IP obligations completed.
- [ ] Payroll/statutory/tax data stored securely and setup initiated.
- [ ] Company identity/email created.
- [ ] MFA enrolled.
- [ ] Access mapped to least-privilege role and approved.
- [ ] Device/equipment issued with custody record.
- [ ] Security and required policy acknowledgements completed.
- [ ] Manager verifies access works and excess privileges are absent.
- [ ] Customer/vendor responsibilities assigned if applicable.
- [ ] Personnel record/index and review dates created.

Do not put completed personnel records containing salary, ID, bank, tax, or signatures into Git.
