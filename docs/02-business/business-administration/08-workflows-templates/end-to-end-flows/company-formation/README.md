# Company Formation — End-to-End Flow

Turns incorporation from a filing task into a complete company-operating setup.

## Trigger
Founders decide to operate through a legal entity or materially restructure the current entity.

## Inputs
Entity/name/capital/shareholder/director/objective decisions, registered address, founder/IP asset inventory, professional legal/accounting advice where needed, identity/KYC documents in secure storage, initial banking/payment requirements, and intended billing/tax setup.

## Flow
```text
Founders decide structure
→ legal/accounting consultation
→ name/entity/capital/director/shareholder/objective decisions
→ incorporation documents
→ signatures/submission
→ DBD registration/result
→ corporate master/registers
→ tax/accounting setup
→ corporate bank/KYC
→ IP/domain/cloud/account ownership alignment
→ payment-provider onboarding
→ invoice/contract/privacy entity particulars updated
→ compliance calendar activated
```

## Completion evidence
Current corporate documents, authority matrix, shareholder/cap-table reconciliation, tax/accounting status, bank setup, provider/KYC status, IP ownership path, domain/account control, safe register links, and recurring compliance dates.

## Exception paths
Registration correction, founder asset not yet assignable, bank/KYC delay, tax registration pending, old personal account still in use, or mismatch between statutory and internal ownership records must remain as named exceptions with owner and target date.
