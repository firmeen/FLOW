# Corporate Templates

Holds safe baselines for board/shareholder resolutions, authority matrices, corporate registers, change checklists, IP assignment schedules, cap-table event coversheets, and KYC/corporate evidence indexes.

## Requirements
Templates must reference the company/legal entity and current governance rules, identify authority/approval source, show effective dates and filing dependencies, and list downstream records/systems to update after a corporate change.

## Control rule
A corporate template is never proof that the corporate action occurred. Executed resolution, official filing/result, statutory register update, and secure evidence link are separate completion requirements.

## Review triggers
Director/shareholder/capital changes, investor/shareholder agreement changes, new bank/provider authority model, corporate-law/professional-advice update, or entity restructuring.
