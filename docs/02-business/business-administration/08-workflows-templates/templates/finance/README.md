# Finance Templates

Holds safe, controlled forms/schemas for quotations, payment requests, reimbursement, reconciliations, AR/AP schedules, close packs, cash reports, and other finance operations.

## Required design elements
Unique ID/period, preparer/reviewer/approver, supporting-document references, amount/currency/tax fields, status, exception section, accounting reference, payment/bank/provider reference where relevant, and completion sign-off.

## Use rule
A template must match the actual accounting process and system fields. Avoid spreadsheet templates that become a hidden second ledger or permit users to bypass controlled numbering/approval systems.

## Review triggers
Accounting-policy change, bank/provider change, new revenue/payment model, tax-registration change, recurring reconciliation issue, new approval threshold, or audit finding.
