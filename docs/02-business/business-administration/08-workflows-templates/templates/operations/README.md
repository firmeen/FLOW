# Administrative Operations Templates

Holds cross-functional forms used to execute and prove recurring business-administration actions that do not belong solely to finance, tax, corporate, HR, privacy, or contracts.

## Examples
Approval/exception request, compliance-calendar action cover sheet, vendor/customer onboarding control sheet, change-impact assessment, service-incident business record, document handover, authority-change checklist, and closure/archival cover sheet.

## Required design
Trigger, owner, inputs, required specialist reviews, decision fields, downstream system/register updates, completion criteria, exception/escalation, evidence references, and reviewer sign-off.

## Rule
Use an operations template to coordinate existing responsibilities, not to invent a shadow process that bypasses the authoritative functional controls.
