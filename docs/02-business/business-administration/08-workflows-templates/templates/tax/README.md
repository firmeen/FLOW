# Tax Templates

Holds safe tax-operation checklists, reconciliation schedules, registers, exception forms, and evidence schemas approved for the company's actual tax status.

## Examples
VAT reconciliation schedule, input-tax exception list, WHT issued/received register schema, tax-filing cover sheet, tax-payment reconciliation, CIT working-paper index, payroll-tax checklist, and authority inquiry tracker.

## Control rule
Do not publish a tax form/template as authoritative merely because it was used in a prior year. Confirm current registration status, entity particulars, tax period, and accountant/tax-adviser requirements.

## Review triggers
Tax registration/status change, legal requirement change, new transaction type, new branch/entity, repeated filing exception, or professional-adviser update.
