# HR & Workforce Templates

Holds safe baselines for hiring approvals, offer/employment documents, job descriptions, confidentiality/IP schedules, access/equipment forms, policy acknowledgements, contractor SOW/acceptance, and offboarding checklists.

## Design rules
Separate employee personal data from the template; identify legal employer/contracting entity; include effective date and approval; link access/equipment tasks to role; use objective contractor acceptance criteria; include return/deletion/handover obligations; and state when legal/HR/accounting review is mandatory.

## Storage boundary
Completed employee/contractor forms containing identity, salary, bank/tax data, signatures, or sensitive HR information belong in restricted personnel/contractor storage, not the source repository.
