# Shareholders' Agreement

Controls the private governance agreement among shareholders and its relationship to statutory/constitutional company rules.

## Common areas
Reserved matters, board rights, information rights, transfer restrictions, pre-emption, future issuance/dilution, tag/drag, founder obligations, investor protections, confidentiality, deadlock, defaults, and exit mechanisms.

## Operating controls
- identify the effective version and all parties;
- map reserved matters to the approval-authority matrix;
- map notice/consent requirements to compliance calendar;
- reconcile provisions with constitutional/statutory records;
- register amendments/joinders/terminations;
- ensure new investors/holders become bound where required.

A shareholder agreement that conflicts with actual corporate records creates operational risk and must be escalated for legal review.
