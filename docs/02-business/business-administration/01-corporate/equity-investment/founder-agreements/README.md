# Founder Agreements

Documents founder roles, contributions, ownership expectations, decision rules, confidentiality/IP, departure scenarios, and contributed assets before ambiguity becomes expensive.

## Required schedules
Explicitly list material contributed or controlled assets such as GitHub repositories, domains, brand/logo files, product/design documentation, cloud accounts, customer introductions/contracts, equipment, cash advances, and pre-existing IP.

## Key decision areas
Roles/time commitment, cash/non-cash contributions, equity/vesting if used, decision authority, expenses/advances, conflicts/outside work, confidentiality, IP assignment, founder departure, transfer restrictions, and dispute/deadlock handling.

## Completion gate
Founder-created assets that the company depends on must have a documented ownership/control path; a private account or personal ownership arrangement must be identified and intentionally resolved.
