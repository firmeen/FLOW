# Share Transfer

Controls transfer of existing ownership between holders.

## Required packet
Transfer agreement/instrument, seller/buyer identity, shares/class, consideration/payment evidence where applicable, required consents/waivers, certificate treatment, shareholder-register update, statutory filing where required, cap-table update, and tax/legal review where relevant.

## Workflow
```text
Transfer proposed → restrictions/pre-emption checked → approvals/waivers → instrument signed → consideration completed → register/certificate action → filing → cap table → archive
```

## Failure modes
Transfer reflected in cap table only; pre-emption/right-of-first-refusal ignored; old certificate remains active without record; payment and transfer dates cannot be reconciled.
