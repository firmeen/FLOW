# Domains & Brand Assets

Controls ownership, custody, renewal, and recovery of domains and master brand assets.

## Domain register
Domain, registrar, registrant/owner, admin account, DNS operator, renewal/expiry date, auto-renewal status, transfer lock, recovery contacts, MFA status, and secure credential-reference location.

## Brand-asset register
Asset name/type, approved master file, creator/source, ownership/licence evidence, approved variants, usage guideline, storage location, and superseded versions.

## Control rules
Prefer company-controlled registrar and storage accounts. Personal founder/employee ownership must be documented as a temporary exception and migrated. Departure/offboarding must review registrar, DNS, social, design, and brand-file access.
