# Intellectual Property

Controls FLOW's chain of title and evidence for code, designs, documents, trademarks, domains, brand assets, and third-party/open-source components.

## Principle
Git history proves contribution history but does not by itself prove the company owns every right. Ownership must be supported by founder, employee, contractor, supplier, or assignment terms as applicable.

## Workflow
```text
Asset/work created or acquired → creator/source identified → ownership/licence terms checked → assignment/registration action if needed → provenance evidence retained → company IP register updated → renewal/licence obligations calendared
```

## Completion gate
Critical product/brand assets must be controlled by the company or covered by an explicit licence/ownership path; personal accounts and undocumented contractor rights are exceptions requiring remediation.
