# Corporate Certificates & Current Particulars

Controls authoritative certificates and current corporate extracts used for KYC, contracting, diligence, banking, payment providers, vendors, and regulatory interactions.

## Register fields
Document type, issuing authority, issue date, freshness requirement, legal entity, registration number, current directors/authority shown, secure storage link, requested-by/use case, and superseded date.

## Operating controls
- distinguish current/fresh copies from historical evidence;
- do not send stale certificates when a counterparty requires a recent extract;
- verify information against the corporate master before external use;
- record who supplied the document and for what purpose when the transmission is material;
- protect documents containing personal identifiers or signatures.

## Trigger
New KYC request, financing/payment-provider onboarding, enterprise customer/vendor diligence, investor diligence, corporate change, or periodic refresh.
