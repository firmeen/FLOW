# Registered Address

Controls the legal registered-office record and all downstream changes caused by moving or correcting the company's registered address.

## Required evidence
Current official address, effective date, authority/approval, filing reference, official confirmation, branch/head-office status where relevant, secure proof documents, and change-notification log.

## Downstream update matrix
Review at minimum: Revenue Department/tax records, bank, payment providers, invoices/tax documents, contracts/order forms, privacy notices, insurance, major vendors, corporate website/legal notices, and regulatory registrations.

## Workflow
```text
Address change approved → evidence of new address obtained → filing prepared/submitted → official result verified → master data changed → every dependent system/template/provider updated → old address record archived
```

## Completion gate
Not complete while any customer-facing financial/legal document or critical KYC system still shows an obsolete address without an intentional transition plan.
