# Incorporation

Stores the operating specification for creating the legal entity and preserving the incorporation evidence chain.

## Inputs
Founder decisions on entity/name, capital, share structure, shareholders, directors, signing authority, registered address, objectives, and professional advice where required.

## Workflow
```text
Structure decided → name/details validated → incorporation pack prepared → signatures/authority checked → submission → registration result verified → certificate/current particulars obtained → corporate master/registers created → accounting/tax/bank/provider onboarding starts
```

## Required evidence
Decision record, submitted forms/documents, signature/authority evidence, official submission/receipt/reference, final registration result, effective date, secure storage link, and downstream setup checklist.

## Failure modes
Only a certificate is saved while the submitted basis is lost; internal cap table does not match legal records; personal founder accounts remain the owner of company assets; bank/tax/provider onboarding uses outdated particulars.
