# Registered Objectives

Controls the company's officially registered business-objective wording and the evidence for any amendment.

## Use
Reference this folder when incorporation or later business expansion requires confirmation that registered objectives support the intended activities.

## Required controls
- current registered-objectives text and effective date;
- historical versions;
- board/shareholder or other approval evidence where applicable;
- filing/submission reference;
- official confirmation of change;
- professional advice for unusual or regulated activities;
- downstream impact review for contracts, licences, bank/payment-provider KYC, investor diligence, and tax/accounting setup.

## Workflow
```text
New/changed activity identified → current objectives reviewed → gap assessed → professional/corporate advice → approval → filing → result verified → corporate master updated → affected third parties updated
```

Do not modify an internal summary and assume the registered objectives changed.
