# Shareholders

Controls the authoritative ownership evidence for shareholders and its reconciliation to the cap table and legal records.

## Required records
Shareholder name/legal identity, share class/type where applicable, quantity, acquisition/transfer/issuance reference, effective date, certificate/register reference, voting/economic rights source, and secure evidence location.

## Reconciliation rule
The management cap table is not the legal source by itself. Reconcile cap-table versions to actual issuance/transfer events and statutory shareholder records.

## Trigger
Incorporation, new share issuance, transfer, cancellation or capital event, investor diligence, annual/statutory update, or discrepancy discovery.

## Failure modes
Spreadsheet ownership differs from legal records; transfer evidence missing; certificates and register inconsistent; investor reporting uses an outdated cap table.
