# Corporate Governance

Controls how directors and shareholders make legally and operationally significant decisions and how FLOW proves who had authority at the time.

## Scope
Director/shareholder records, board and shareholder resolutions, signing/delegation authority, material decision evidence, conflicts where relevant, and synchronization with statutory records.

## Workflow
```text
Matter identified → required decision body/authority determined → conflicts/quorum/process checked → materials prepared → decision made → resolution/minutes/evidence finalized → filing or delegated action performed → registers/authority matrix updated → historical evidence archived
```

## Control rule
Important decisions must not exist only in chat, email, or memory. The record should show entity, date, participants, decision, delegated authority, effective date, signatures/evidence, and resulting actions.
