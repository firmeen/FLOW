# Board Resolutions

Controls board decisions and written resolutions for matters requiring or benefiting from formal board authority.

## Typical triggers
Bank-account actions, material contracts, borrowing/guarantees, major vendor commitments, officer appointments, share-related actions, significant asset/IP transactions, annual/statutory approvals, or delegated authority changes.

## Required structure
Company identity, resolution ID, meeting/written method, date, participants, quorum/process basis where relevant, conflicts, matter considered, exact decision, delegated authority, conditions, effective date, signatures/evidence, resulting filing/action, and register link.

## Workflow
```text
Matter prepared → legal/finance/business impact reviewed → board materials distributed → decision → resolution finalized → action delegated/performed → filing/provider update if needed → resolution register updated
```

Never treat a draft resolution as effective evidence.
