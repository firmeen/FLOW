# Shareholder Resolutions

Controls decisions reserved to or requiring shareholders under law, constitutional documents, or shareholder arrangements.

## Typical matters
Capital/share changes, certain director matters, amendments to constitutional documents, reserved matters, major transactions, restructuring, and other statutory approvals.

## Required evidence
Resolution ID, company/shareholder identities, voting/consent basis, date, required threshold, notices/waivers if relevant, exact resolutions, conditions/effective date, signatures/acceptance evidence, filing obligations, and cap-table/register consequences.

## Control rule
Before acting, reconcile the proposed decision with the current shareholders' agreement, constitutional/statutory rules, and cap table. After action, update every ownership and corporate record affected.
