# Annual and Recurring Compliance Calendar

## Purpose
FLOW needs a calendar that turns legal, accounting, tax, corporate, payroll, contract, privacy, security, and vendor obligations into scheduled work with evidence of completion.

## Calendar categories

### Monthly / periodic finance
- bookkeeping close;
- bank reconciliation;
- payment-provider reconciliation;
- accounts receivable review;
- accounts payable review;
- payroll close;
- VAT filing/payment if applicable;
- withholding-tax filing/payment as applicable;
- statutory payroll/social-security obligations;
- management financial review.

### Annual corporate/accounting
- year-end close;
- financial statements;
- audit process where required;
- corporate income-tax process;
- DBD financial-statement/corporate filings as applicable;
- annual shareholder/director approvals/meetings as required;
- auditor/accountant engagement renewal;
- shareholder/corporate records review.

### Contract lifecycle
- customer renewal notice dates;
- discount expiry;
- vendor renewal/cancellation windows;
- payment-provider fee/contract review;
- insurance renewal if obtained;
- lease/domain/trademark renewals.

### Privacy/security
- Privacy Notice/DPA review;
- subprocessor review;
- ROPA update;
- data-retention review;
- access review;
- disaster-recovery/backup test;
- incident-response exercise;
- policy review.

## Monthly close sequence
A practical monthly calendar should sequence tasks rather than setting every item on the same day:

```text
Month ends
→ collect source documents
→ close billing
→ reconcile bank/provider
→ close AP/AR
→ payroll/tax schedules
→ VAT/WHT preparation
→ accounting adjustments
→ management review
→ filings/payments
→ archive evidence
```

## Year-end sequence

```text
Year-end cut-off planning
→ inventory of missing documents
→ bank/provider confirmations
→ AR/AP review
→ fixed assets
→ payroll/statutory reconciliation
→ tax schedules
→ accountant closes books
→ audit/financial statement process
→ director/shareholder approvals as required
→ tax/DBD filings
→ final signed statements and receipts archived
→ next-year opening balances verified
```

## Event-driven compliance
Not everything is periodic. Create immediate tasks when:
- director changes;
- address changes;
- branch opens/closes;
- capital/shareholder changes;
- bank signatory changes;
- VAT status changes;
- new employee starts/leaves;
- material vendor/subprocessor starts;
- material data incident occurs;
- major contract is signed/terminated;
- new payment flow changes regulatory position.

## Escalation
For deadlines, define:
- first reminder;
- preparer deadline before statutory deadline;
- reviewer deadline;
- escalation owner;
- contingency if adviser/source document is delayed.

Never set the internal deadline equal to the final statutory deadline.

## Evidence of completion
A calendar item is not complete because somebody checked a box. Attach or reference:
- return/file submitted;
- submission acknowledgment;
- payment receipt;
- signed minutes/resolution;
- reconciliation report;
- review approval;
- exception explanation.

## Common failures
- accountant owns deadlines mentally with no internal visibility;
- renewal notice missed because only invoice date was tracked;
- filing completed but receipt not retained;
- annual privacy/security review exists on paper but no evidence;
- internal due dates leave no review buffer.
