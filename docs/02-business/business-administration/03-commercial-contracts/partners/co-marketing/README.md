# Co-Marketing Agreements

Controls joint campaigns, events, content, lead collection, logos/brand use, costs, and data sharing with partners.

## Required decisions
Campaign scope/dates, channels, brand approvals, content/IP ownership and reuse, claims/representations, budget/cost split, lead capture fields, consent/privacy notices, lead sharing and follow-up rights, analytics, confidentiality, third-party vendors, cancellation, and post-campaign asset/data handling.

## Workflow
```text
Campaign brief → partner/brand/data roles → budget/legal/privacy review → approvals → content production → pre-launch QA → campaign → lead/data handling → performance reporting → invoice/settlement → data retention/deletion → close
```

No partner should receive lead/personal data merely because logos appear on the same campaign; data-sharing purpose and notice/consent basis must be explicit.
