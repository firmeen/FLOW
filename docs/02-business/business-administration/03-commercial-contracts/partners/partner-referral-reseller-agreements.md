# Partners, Referrals, Resellers and Implementation Partners

## Purpose
The word “partner” is commercially vague. This document family forces FLOW to define the actual legal and operational relationship before giving another party authority, commissions, branding rights, customer access, or data access.

## Relationship types

### Referral partner
Introduces prospects but does not normally bind FLOW.

Documents should define:
- qualified lead definition;
- registration/attribution process;
- commission trigger;
- commission rate/base;
- payment timing;
- exclusions and duplicate leads;
- refund/cancellation clawback;
- no authority to bind FLOW;
- marketing statements allowed;
- confidentiality and data handling.

### Reseller
May sell FLOW subscriptions under agreed commercial rules.

Define:
- territory/customer segment;
- pricing/discount authority;
- whether reseller contracts in its own name or facilitates FLOW contract;
- who invoices customer;
- who collects money;
- tax responsibility;
- first-line support;
- customer ownership/transition after termination;
- brand usage;
- prohibited promises;
- reporting/audit rights;
- data access.

### Implementation partner
Helps deploy/configure FLOW.

Define:
- certification/competency requirements;
- permitted system access;
- customer authorization;
- implementation SOW ownership;
- responsibility for errors/misconfiguration;
- support handoff;
- confidentiality/security;
- customer data use;
- IP/customization rules.

### Integration partner
Connects systems/APIs.

Define:
- API use and scope;
- credentials;
- data flows;
- security obligations;
- uptime/dependency boundaries;
- ownership of connectors;
- incident notification;
- termination and credential revocation.

## Commission evidence chain

```text
Lead registered
→ eligibility verified
→ customer contract becomes effective
→ invoice/payment threshold achieved
→ commission calculation
→ partner statement/invoice as applicable
→ tax/WHT review
→ approval
→ payment
→ accounting/reconciliation
```

Do not pay commission from a salesperson's informal spreadsheet with no link to collected customer revenue.

## Brand authorization
Maintain a brand-use schedule defining:
- approved logos/assets;
- permitted descriptions;
- prohibited claims;
- approval process for campaigns;
- termination/removal obligation.

## Customer data boundary
A partner should receive only data required for its role. If an implementation/reseller partner handles personal data, privacy/DPA/data-sharing analysis is required.

## Termination flow

```text
termination trigger/notice
→ stop new lead/customer authority
→ calculate final commissions
→ transfer active customer cases
→ remove brand usage
→ revoke system/API access
→ return/delete confidential and personal data
→ settle outstanding amounts
→ notify affected customers where needed
→ archive termination evidence
```

## Common failures
- referral partner tells customer it can legally commit FLOW;
- unclear commission calculation;
- reseller collects money but tax/payment responsibility is undefined;
- partner retains customer data after termination;
- partner uses outdated logo/pricing;
- customer support responsibility is unclear.
