# Reseller Agreements

Controls partners that resell or commercially represent FLOW and clarifies whether the reseller buys/resells or merely intermediates.

## Required model decisions
Customer contracting party, authority to quote, product/territory, pricing/discount/margin, collections, taxes, support/onboarding, customer data access, branding, renewal, refunds, liabilities, customer ownership, and transition at termination.

## Operational controls
Approved price catalogue, entitlement/order validation, named partner admins, customer-contract evidence, settlement/reconciliation, prohibited representations, training/version updates, and escalation contacts.

## Completion gate
FLOW must be able to identify the end customer, active entitlements, governing customer terms, money flow, and party responsible for support/privacy obligations.
