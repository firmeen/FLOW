# Referral Agreements

Controls introductions where a partner refers potential customers to FLOW.

## Terms to define
Eligible lead, lead-registration method, duplicate/existing-account rule, territory/segment if any, referral ownership period, commission trigger, excluded taxes/refunds/fees, payment timing, refund/clawback, reporting, prohibited representations, confidentiality, data sharing, term, and termination tail.

## Workflow
```text
Partner approved → agreement → lead registered → duplicate/eligibility check → opportunity tracked → customer contract/payment trigger → commission calculation → finance review → settlement → reconciliation/reporting
```

## Evidence
Referral ID, partner, customer/opportunity, accepted registration time, revenue basis, eligible amount, commission calculation, payment, adjustments/clawbacks, and dispute notes.
