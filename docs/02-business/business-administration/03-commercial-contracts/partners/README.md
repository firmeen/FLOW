# Partner Agreements

Controls referral, reseller, implementation, integration, revenue-sharing, and co-marketing relationships where responsibilities span organizations.

## Common controls
Partner legal identity, relationship type, authority to represent FLOW, customer ownership, pricing/commission rules, data access, branding, support/implementation responsibilities, confidentiality, IP, billing/tax/payment flow, performance reporting, audit rights, renewal, and termination transition.

## Workflow
```text
Partner model defined → commercial/operational/data responsibilities mapped → finance/privacy/security/legal review → approval → agreement → onboarding/access → performance/settlement → renewal/change → termination/customer transition
```

## Boundary
A partner must not be allowed to promise features, pricing, compliance, or contractual terms outside its documented authority.
