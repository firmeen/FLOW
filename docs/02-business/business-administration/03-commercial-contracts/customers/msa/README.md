# Master Services Agreement (MSA)

Controls the durable legal framework for customer relationships so recurring commercial variables can remain in Order Forms rather than rewriting the core agreement.

## Clause ownership map
At minimum map: billing/tax → Finance; service/SLA/support → Operations/Engineering; data deletion/privacy → Privacy/Engineering; security/incident notice → Security/Privacy/Legal; IP → Product/Legal; renewal/suspension/termination → Sales/Management/Finance.

## Key sections
Parties/definitions, access rights and restrictions, fees/taxes/billing, customer data/content, confidentiality, IP, privacy/DPA linkage, third-party services, service commitments, suspension, term/termination, post-termination data, warranties/disclaimers, liability/indemnity where professionally advised, governing law/disputes, notices, and order of precedence.

## Non-standard negotiation control
Track clause, customer request, standard position, business/technical risk, legal recommendation, approved fallback, final wording, approver, and downstream operating owner.

## Completion gate
An executed MSA is incomplete until the contract register, obligations, Order Form linkage, DPA/SLA dependencies, and renewal/termination rules are activated.
