# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Defines measurable service commitments rather than general marketing expectations.

## Required components
Covered service/scope, availability or response metric, measurement period and source, exclusions, planned/emergency maintenance treatment, severity classification, response/update/restoration targets, customer notification process, service-credit/remedy mechanism if offered, claim procedure, and escalation.

## Operating evidence
Monitoring records, incident start/end times, affected service/tenant scope, excluded events, communications, remediation timeline, and monthly/period calculation where an uptime metric is promised.

## Validation before signing
Confirm actual architecture and staffing can meet the proposed metric, incident notification timing, measurement method, support channel, and remedy process. Non-standard enterprise SLA terms require explicit operational/engineering approval.

## Completion gate
An SLA cannot be administered reliably if FLOW lacks the telemetry needed to calculate it consistently.
