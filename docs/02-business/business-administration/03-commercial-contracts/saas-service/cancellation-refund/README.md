# Cancellation & Refund Policy

Controls when a customer may cancel and when money, credits, service access, tax/accounting records, and data obligations change.

## Policy dimensions
Eligibility, notice/cut-off, committed term, unused/prepaid period, one-time fees, implementation work, discounts, partial periods, payment-provider limits, refund method/timing, non-refundable items, manager exceptions, and taxes/credit notes.

## Workflow
```text
Request/event → customer/order/payment identified → contract/policy eligibility → service usage/status → amount calculation → approval → billing stop/adjustment → provider/bank refund or credit → accounting/tax correction → customer notice → reconciliation → access/data actions if termination → close
```

Order cancellation, subscription cancellation, payment refund, credit note, and account termination are linked but distinct events and must not be collapsed into one status.
