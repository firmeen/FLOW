# Cash Management

Controls liquidity visibility, cash movements, short-term obligations, and approved use of cash without replacing formal accounting records.

## Typical artifacts
Cash-position report, upcoming payment schedule, receivables collection view, runway/liquidity forecast, restricted/earmarked cash note, inter-account transfer approval, petty-cash controls if used, and unusual cash-movement exception log.

## Workflow
```text
Opening balances reconciled → expected inflows/outflows collected → near-term obligations prioritized → funding/transfer needs approved → payments executed → actual bank movement confirmed → forecast updated → exceptions escalated
```

## Controls
Forecasts must reconcile back to bank/accounting balances; transfers require purpose and authority; personal and company funds must not be mixed without documented accounting treatment; founder/shareholder advances must be separately identifiable.
