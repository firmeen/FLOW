# Bank Reconciliation

Reconciles bank-statement reality to the accounting ledger and identifies timing, classification, duplication, omission, or unauthorized-transaction differences.

## Required fields
Account, period, statement opening/closing balance, ledger balance, unreconciled receipts/payments, bank fees/interest, transfers, outstanding items, unexplained differences, preparer, reviewer, completion date, and adjustment references.

## Workflow
```text
Statement obtained → opening balance agreed → transactions matched → differences classified → missing entries posted → duplicates/errors investigated → closing balance agreed → reviewer sign-off → exceptions carried with owner
```

## Completion gate
A reconciliation with unexplained balancing entries is not complete. Material old outstanding items require investigation and escalation, not permanent carry-forward.
