# Corporate Income Tax

Builds the year-end CIT support file throughout the year rather than reconstructing it from bank statements.

## Working-file areas
Revenue completeness, accruals/prepayments, deductible and non-deductible/restricted expenses, fixed assets/depreciation, bad debts/write-offs, related parties, foreign/vendor payments, WHT credits, prior-year balances/losses, tax adjustments/provisions, and reconciliation to financial statements.

## Workflow
```text
Monthly accounting close → tax-sensitive items flagged → schedules maintained → year-end trial balance → tax reconciliation/adjustments → adviser/accountant review → return → approval → filing/payment → accounting/control-account reconciliation → archive
```

Exact tax positions must be based on current law and professional review.
