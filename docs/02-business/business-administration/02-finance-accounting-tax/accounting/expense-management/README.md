# Expense Management

Controls employee/founder reimbursements and business expenses so personal and company spending remain distinguishable and supported.

## Expense claim fields
Claimant, date, merchant/vendor, business purpose, project/customer if relevant, amount/currency, receipt/invoice, payment method, tax document quality, accounting code, approver, reimbursement status, and payment reference.

## Workflow
```text
Expense incurred/requested → evidence captured → business purpose confirmed → policy/limit check → tax/accounting review where needed → approval → reimbursement/payment → ledger posting → reconciliation
```

## Rules
Do not reimburse unsupported material expenses without a documented exception. Personal spend accidentally paid by company or company spend paid personally must use a defined correction/advance/reimbursement treatment approved by finance/accounting.
