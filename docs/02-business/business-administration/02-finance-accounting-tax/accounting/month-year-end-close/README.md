# Month & Year-End Close

Turns period-end accounting into a repeatable checklist and evidence pack rather than a later reconstruction.

## Monthly sequence
```text
Cut-off inputs → customer invoices/credits → vendor invoices/expenses → bank reconciliation → payment-provider reconciliation → AR/AP → VAT/WHT/payroll inputs → accruals/prepayments → fixed assets → unusual-balance review → management reports → accountant review → adjustment log → period close
```

## Close pack
Reconciliation summaries, outstanding exceptions, adjusting journals, tax filing references, fixed-asset/AR/AP schedules, reviewer approval, final trial balance/management reports, and carry-forward action list.

## Year-end additions
Annual financial-statement support, tax reconciliation, statutory/audit requirements, confirmation of opening/closing balances, and archive of final approved statements/returns.

A period is not cleanly closed while material reconciliations remain unexplained.
