# Bookkeeping

Controls routine recording of source-supported transactions and the audit trail from ledger entry back to business evidence.

## Required source packet
Reference the underlying contract/order/SOW/PO, invoice or receipt, delivery/acceptance evidence, payment/bank/provider reference, tax/WHT/VAT evidence where applicable, business purpose, and approval.

## Controls
- record in the correct period;
- use approved chart-of-accounts mapping;
- separate gross amounts, taxes, fees, refunds, and settlements appropriately;
- prevent duplicate posting using source identifiers;
- identify manual journals and reviewer;
- preserve correction/reversal history.

## Completion gate
A bank transaction with no business classification or source support remains an accounting exception, even if the cash movement itself is clear.
