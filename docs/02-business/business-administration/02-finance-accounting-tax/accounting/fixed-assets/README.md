# Fixed Assets

Maintains evidence and accounting control for laptops, equipment, office assets, servers, or other capitalized property.

## Asset register fields
Asset ID, description/category, vendor/invoice, acquisition date/cost, serial number, custodian/location, accounting account, depreciation policy/reference, useful-life information from accountant policy, status, transfer/repair history, disposal date/method, and proceeds where applicable.

## Lifecycle
```text
Purchase approved → invoice/payment → asset recognized → ID/tag assigned → custody recorded → periodic verification → repair/transfer → disposal approval → sale/scrap evidence → accounting removal → archive
```

## Control rule
Employee/contractor offboarding must reconcile issued company assets. Disposal cannot mean simply deleting the asset row.
