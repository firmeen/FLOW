# Tax Invoices

Controls tax-invoice templates, issuance, required particulars, numbering, and adjustment linkage according to FLOW's actual VAT registration and current legal particulars.

## Controls
- only use accountant-reviewed templates applicable to current registration status;
- seller branch/address/tax particulars must match approved master data;
- document number/date must be controlled;
- taxable value and VAT calculation must reconcile to sales records;
- original and adjustment documents remain linked;
- manual edits to required fields require controlled review;
- tax-invoice exception/correction cases are tracked.

## Completion gate
Issued tax invoices must reconcile to the output VAT register/accounting and be included in the appropriate period-control process.
